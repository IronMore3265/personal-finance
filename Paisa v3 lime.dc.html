Prototype · v3 · lime

Paisa

Local-first finance tracker for multi-account, multi-currency spending. Android via Capacitor, real SQLite on device.

What changed in v3
One family — Manrope, tabular numerals — with weight and size doing the work instead of borders.
Soft-white cards on light grey, 20–26px radius, low-spread shadows. Borders only where a divider is needed.
Lime is the single accent: fills, chips and the balance delta. Gains read in green, never in lime text.
Home leads with the total balance, then one black card for the primary account with its two live actions.
Live in this prototype
Log a transaction on the numpad · mark a bill paid (auto-drafts it) · contribute to a goal · paste an SMS and run the rule table · filter and search activity · flip light/dark from the header.
Still open
Transfers: own flow or third type on the same sheet? · Per-account budgets as well as per-category? · Should the USD account show in BDT on Home, or its own currency? · v1 calm version is kept as a separate file.
